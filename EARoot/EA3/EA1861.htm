Training and development needs, that are aligned to the company’s strategic objectives, are as follows: <br/>liaison with managers and with reference to the job profiles of staff; <br/>through informal discussions which Heads of Departments hold with each staff member about their development and professional development needs; and  <br/>through the implementation of the Development Cycle, including the development conversation with the outcome of a development plan for each staff member which will play an important role in identifying of individual development needs.  This personal development plans will be collated into a training plan for each Department and Division, which in turn will be collated into an institutional (i.e., Group and respective subsidiaries) training and development plan.<br/><br/>
