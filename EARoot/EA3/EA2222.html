<b>Risks can be viewed from three distinct perspectives:</b><br/>Risk as an opportunity: Viewing risk from the opportunity perspective recognizes the inherent relationship between risk and return. Managing risk as an opportunity necessitates actions being taken by business managers to achieve positive gains.  Opportunity analysis creates insights that may be used by business managers to increase the likelihood of success, and decrease the likelihood of failure.<br/>Risk as an uncertainty: When considering risks from this perspective, business units must determine how they can be proactive in preventing uncertain future events from having a negative impact. The management of uncertainty seeks to ensure that a business unit’s actual performance falls within a defined range.  The management of uncertainty risk is proactive – one must anticipate the impact of change and establish controls/processes designed to mitigate its effect on the operations of the business unit.<br/>Risk as a hazard: Risks may be viewed as the possibility of a negative event taking place, or the fact that the negative event has taken place.  Such potential negative events include financial loss, fraud, theft and damage to assets.<br/>Emerging Risk Identification: Conduct a focused session to identify, assess, and log any new or emerging risks that have developed within your operational environment over the last 8 months taking into consideration the court order, winding down period, budget reduction and restructuring, Non-compliances received from DFFE, Poor or Ineffective Stock and document Management, Relations with Partners and Landowners, planned vs actual production, Business continuity, Sufficient Training opportunities).<br/>Risk Status: Verify if any risks have materialized, been successfully mitigated (and closed), or if their overall status has changed.<br/>New Risks Identified: Document any risks that have emerged since the last formal review.   (court order, winding down period, budget reduction and restructuring, Non-compliances received from DFFE, Poor or Ineffective Stock and document Management, Relations with Partners and Landowners, planned vs actual production, Business continuity, Sufficient Training opportunities).<br/>Mitigation Progress: Provide concrete progress updates on all active mitigation and contingency plans.<br/>Residual Risk Scoring: Re-assess and update the current Likelihood and Impact scores based on the effectiveness of existing controls.<br/><b>Qualitative Risk Analysis: A quick, subjective assessment to prioritize risks.</b><br/>Probability: How likely is the risk to occur? (e.g., scale of 1-5, Low/High).<br/>Impact: If it occurs, how severe will the effect be on the project's scope, schedule, budget, or quality? (e.g., scale of 1-5, Low/High).<br/>Risk Score: Multiply Probability x Impact to get a numerical score (e.g., 3x4=12).<br/><br/><br/>
