Interview panel<br/><br/>Line management shall be responsible for the selection of the interview panel.<br/><br/>The interview panel must comprise of a minimum of 4 members.<br/><br/>An HCM representative must from part of the panel.<br/><br/>An employment equity representative must form part of the panel.<br/><br/>Line management is responsible for the final decision on the selection of the candidate to be appointed.<br/><br/>The interview panel must ensure selection of candidate complies with the requirements of the vacancy and with employment equity goals.<br/><br/>All panel members must complete and sign the declaration form to indicate if they have any knowledge and or relationship with any of the interviewed candidates.<br/><br/>It is compulsory for all panel members to complete and sign the interview questionnaire for each candidate.<br/><br/>Job Interview and Structuring of Questions<br/><br/>The following provisions apply to the process of interviews:<br/><br/>All short-listed candidates must be invited to an interview in telephonically and in writing. <br/><br/>All interviews must be structured. Questions asked during the interview should be related to job requirements. All job specific questionnaires should be drafted by the relevant line manager with support  from HCM. <br/><br/>The proposed questions must be submitted to HCM as part of the interview pack.<br/><br/>The Interview Panel must be careful not to create any expectations during the interview. <br/><br/>No one, including the panel members of the interview may inform an interviewed candidate of the outcome of an interview before before  an offer has been made. <br/><br/>Where possible, at least two (2) candidates in order of preference must be recommended for a specific post, in order to avoid the post being re-advertised should the first candidate decline the job offer. If the offer is rejected, the line manager, in consultation with the designated HCM functionary, will take a decision either to appoint the next-best candidate or to repeat the recruitment process.<br/><br/>The interview panel shall make a recommendation on the suitability of a candidate after considering:<br/><br/>i. Information based on valid methods, criteria or instruments for selection that are free from any bias or discrimination;<br/>ii. The training, skills, competence and knowledge necessary to meet the inherent requirements of the post;<br/><br/>iii. The needs for the company for developing human resources;<br/><br/>iv. The Employment Equity of the company and office where the post is located; and<br/><br/>v. The Employment Equity Plan of the company.<br/><br/>vi. Preferance to be given to SA citizens. A foreign national can be considered if he/she meets all the job requirements and have valid work permit .<br/>
