The criteria below are used to assess the effectiveness of controls in place to manage the risks which have been identified.  For each risk, you will assess the effectiveness of controls on a current state basis as well assess a target control effectiveness.  The results of the control effectiveness ratings will be used to calculate the current risk status or residual risk as well as the desired risk status or desired residual risk. <br/>A gap between current risk status and desired risk status indicates that steps should be taken to improve the controls in place to manage risks<br/><a href="file://G:\My Drive\Inemuri\Kishugu\Audi Risk Compliance\Risk control.png"><font color="#0000ff"><u>See table</u></font></a><br/><br/>Once the inherent risks have been evaluated using the Risk Matrix and the effectiveness of controls measured to derive residual risk exposure, WOF will determine treatment options for each risk rating based on the definitions provided below.  On a very rare basis, exceptions may be noted.  Exceptions rated as “Very High”, which cannot be eliminated will be handled on a case by case basis by the MD and/or the Board of Directors, through escalation by the Risk Owners.<br/><a href="file://G:\My Drive\Inemuri\Kishugu\Audi Risk Compliance\Risk considerations.png"><font color="#0000ff"><u>See tabl</u></font></a>e<br/><br/>Monitor for risk triggers—early warning signs that a risk is about to occur<br/>Update the risk register with any changes in probability, impact, or status<br/>Close out risks that are no longer relevant or have passed.<br/>
