Include contracting <br/><br/>Transfers<br/><br/>HCM shall be responsible for facilitating all intercompany and interdepartmental transfers.<br/><br/>An employee may be transferred from one company to another as part of a recruitment process.<br/><br/>HCM department must inform the employee’s new line manager in writing of the appointment of the transferred employee and/or successful candidate.<br/><br/>An employee may be transferred to another department within the Group due to operational requirements as defined by the Labour Relations Act (LRA) to be economical, technological, structural or similar needs of the employer. This shall be based on moving an employee in same position or grade within the same company, department or different location.<br/><br/>No employee may be transferred to a different department without following the normal recruitment process. <br/><br/>Probation (6 months)<br/><br/>The employment of new employees is subject to a<b> six (6)  month </b>probation period.  This must be reflected in the job offer.  Probation periods may be extended, within reason, where the employer is not convinced that the employee is performing to the required standard.<br/><br/>During the probationary period, the Line Manager must monitor the new employee’s ability to perform a particular job at a particular level. Supervisors are responsible for the day-to-day supervision of employees on probation through training, offering guidance and advice and monitoring progress. The probationary system is linked to the performance management system.<br/><br/>After the probation period has been successfully completed a confirmation of employment letter will be issued to the employee.<br/><br/>In the event of unsatisfactory work performance during the probation period, the Line Manager must immediately implement a performance improvement plan. If the performance does not improve, the matter should be referred to the HCM department for activating the appropriate incapacity processes. <br/><br/>Possible actions are the extension of the probation period or termination thereof. Termination of probation can happen during the probation period as long as the formal dismissal procedures were followed. When dismissal is considered, the employee must be afforded the opportunity to state his/ her case.<br/><br/>Induction<br/><br/>All employees will undergo induction within the first two weeks of employment. <br/><br/>HCM shall be responsible for scheduling and coordinating the induction programme.<br/><br/>The line manager is responsible for ensuring that the employee completes the induction programme.<br/>The employee must sign the induction acknowledgement form<br/><br/>
