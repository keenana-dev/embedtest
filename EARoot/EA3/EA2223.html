<b>Risk Rating Criteria</b><br/>For each of the risks, you are required to provide an inherent assessment of the impact of the risk on the business, the likelihood or probability of the risk occurring, the current and desired control effectiveness assessment which will be aligned to the organisation’s risk management methodology.  We will use Google Sheets to facilitate the assessment of each risk and the results will be the agreed group score (consensus) using the rating criteria.<br/><b>Likelihood</b><br/>When assessing the likelihood of the risk materialising, you need to consider the inherent possibility that the given event or risk will occur, ignoring any actions management might have taken to mitigate the risk or reduce the probability of the risk materialising. Likelihood will be assessed on a scale of one to five.<br/><br/>Using the risk scores from the analysis, you create a prioritized list. This helps the team focus its energy and resources on the most critical threats and opportunities.<br/>How to do it:<br/>Create a Probability-Impact (P-I) Matrix to visually plot risks.<br/>Create a "Top 10 Risks" list for reporting and management attention.<br/>Define thresholds (e.g., any risk with a score over 15 is "High Priority").<br/><a href="file://G:\My Drive\Inemuri\Kishugu\Audi Risk Compliance\Risk scoring.png"><font color="#0000ff"><b><u>See linked Table</u></b></font></a><br/>
