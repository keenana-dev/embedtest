When assessing the potential impact of the risk, you need to consider to what extent that risk will have an impact on the business.  You need assess impact on an inherent basis therefore ignoring any actions management might have taken to mitigate the risk or reduce the impact of the risk on the business.<br/><br/>“Impact can be defined as the material loss to the organisation, should that risk materialise.”<br/><br/>Create a Probability-Impact (P-I) Matrix to visually plot risks.<br/>Create a "Top 10 Risks" list for reporting and management attention.<br/>Define thresholds (e.g., any risk with a score over 15 is "High Priority").<br/><a href="file://G:\My Drive\Inemuri\Kishugu\Audi Risk Compliance\Risk impact scoring.png"><font color="#0000ff"><u>See table</u></font></a><br/>
