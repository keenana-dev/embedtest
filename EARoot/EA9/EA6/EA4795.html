Kishugu Fleet Solutions (Pty) Ltd (KFS) primary focus within the Kishugu JV sphere is Operational excellence. This is due to the nature of the relationship between KFS and Working on Fire, where cost saving is essential to deliver a fully inclusive fleet service during the 5-year defined budget period. Only variance in the budget is the annual CPIX increase and variable cost recoupment. <br/><br/>KFS, on Non WoF business primary focus will be customer intimacy, where we focus on the personal service to deliver service to the client via our leasing options. The product leadership and operational excellence also plays a big part to retain these clients. <br/><br/>Secondary focus will be regulatory, as in both WoF and Non WoF business, the lease, transport business is highly regulated, and compliance is of utmost importance. Monitoring of these activities are critical to our success. <br/><br/>In terms of social responsibility, KFS strives to partake in the commitment to build the economy and to create jobs for the unemployed, thereby giving back to the community.<br/><br/><b>KFS North Star:</b><br/>Within the Kishugu WoF JV:<br/><br/>Operational excellence - 45%<br/>Regulatory - 25%<br/>Customer Intimacy – 15%<br/>Social responsibility – 10<br/>Product leadership - 5% <br/><br/><b>Closing</b><br/>Following this strategy, the fleet leasing company can work towards its north star vision of become a leader in sustainable and technology technologically advanced fleet leasing while achieving specific measurable achievable relevant and time bound goals to track progress and success regular evaluation and adaption will be essential to stay aligned with the company's vision.<br/>
