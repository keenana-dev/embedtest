Appears In: Set performance goals (individual and team) and align to organisational goals
All employees must ensure that their performance goals for the year are developed and loaded into the HRIS by no later than the end of April of each year. Goals could be towards introducing a new process, system, idea etc or it could be to improve efficiencies within what the employee is expected to achieve. Employees shall be encouraged to develop challenging (‘audacious’) goals which will assist in keeping employees stimulated and innovative. Annual goals for each employee shall be classified as follows:<br/><br/><b>Core Responsibilities Goal (“Keeping the Lights On” Goal)</b><br/><ul>
<li>This is as outlined in the employee’s job description, in other words, what the employee is hired/employed to do. </li><li>This does not need to be a SMART goal as this is the employee’s ongoing work.</li></ul>
<br/><b>2 – 3 Business Goals</b><br/><ul>
<li>This will be major contributions to the employee’s department and/or the organisation’s success.</li><li>These goals must be SMART</li><li>The goals must link to either the unit or department or organisational goals.</li><li>Each goal MUST contain key milestones that needs to be met in order to complete each goal and determine the timeline for completion.</li></ul>
<br/><b>1 – 2 Professional Development Goals </b><br/><ul>
<li>This must be SMART goals describing what professional development the employee targets to achieve in order to improve or sustain good performance.</li><li>These should be in line with the employee’s growth within the organisation.</li><li>These could either be knowledge based or abilities based.</li><li>This could be through formal and informal training and development interventions.</li><li>Capacity assessments must be conducted for all employees and results thereof can be used to identify skills gap. Skills gap identified should be captured as professional development goals for the employee.</li></ul><p/>
