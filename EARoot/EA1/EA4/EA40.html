The future funding stream for the<br/>company’s growth cannot rely solely on the current government funding via the<br/>Department of Forestry, Fisheries and the Environment (the WoF Programme). WOF<br/>needs to diversify its funding streams to ensure resilience and company growth<br/>within the current constrained fiscal environment and macroeconomic context. A detailed strategy must include sourcing alternative government funds, private sector and foundation sponsorship, multilateral and development agency funding,<br/>leveraging commercial IFM activities and international deployments; while exploring the monetization of select services. This should also focus on developing and implementing similar youth focussed public employment initiatives, in other sectors re-packaging the IFMS and HAT models into scalable solutions and developing commercial service offerings and public benefit projects that pursue the goals of environmental restoration.<br/>
